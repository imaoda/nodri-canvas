A **canvas operating system for creative work**. Organize assets, connect ideas, and create with AI in one place.

## Your files. On your drive.

Drag local files onto the canvas. Imported media and canvas-generated results stay in your project. **Create across platforms; manage locally.** Reuse assets as references across providers without relying on temporary web links.

## More nodes. Still in flow.

Designed for creative canvases with **hundreds of nodes**, with responsive pan, zoom, selection, and drag. See the real app in motion below.

## Switch platforms. Keep your workflow.

For short dramas and vertical videos, connect **characters → scenes → storyboards → clips**. Use the same canvas experience across providers: connect nodes or use **@ references**, write a prompt, choose a model, and build on the result.

## Your first creation, in three steps

Install Nodri, create a project, and add a generation account from the toolbar.

1. **Bring references.** Drag in character and scene images. Double-click empty canvas space to create an image or video node.
2. **Describe the shot.** Connect references or use `@` in its generation input. Describe framing and action.
3. **Generate.** Choose a model, check the settings, and generate a storyboard image or clip.
